📖 How to get your TNG statement text
  1. Open TNG eWallet app → tap Profile → Transaction History
  2. Tap the email icon — TNG will email you a PDF statement
  3. Open the PDF, select all text (Ctrl+A), copy and paste below
  4. Alternatively use the TNG ePortal at ewallet.tngdigital.com.my

📖 How to export from UOB Personal Internet Banking
  1. Log in at pib.uob.com.my
  2. Go to Account → Account Activities
  3. Select your account and date range (up to 90 days)
  4. Click Download → choose Excel (XLS) or CSV
  5. Upload the downloaded file below
